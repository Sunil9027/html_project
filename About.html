Hello! I'm Sunil Kumar

I'm Sunil Kumar, an Information Technology graduate and aspiring Java Full Stack Developer passionate about software development, problem-solving, and building practical web applications.

I completed my B.Tech in Information Technology from Rajkiya Engineering College, Banda, under Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU) in 2026, with a CGPA of 7.75. I also hold a Diploma in Computer Science and Engineering, which helped me build my foundation in computer science and software development.

Currently, I am focusing on strengthening my skills in Java Full Stack Development through technical training at JSpiders, Rajajinagar, Bengaluru, and hands-on practice. My technical interests include Java programming, Object-Oriented Programming (OOP), Data Structures, SQL, MySQL, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, JDBC, Servlets, JSP, Hibernate, and modern backend technologies.

I enjoy understanding how applications work behind the scenes, from designing user-friendly interfaces to implementing backend logic and connecting databases. I believe in writing clean, maintainable code, understanding programming fundamentals, and continuously improving my problem-solving abilities.

Beyond technical skills, I am working on improving my communication, logical thinking, and ability to explain technical concepts clearly. I believe that consistent practice, curiosity, and a willingness to learn are essential for becoming a successful software developer.

My career goal is to become a skilled Java Full Stack Developer, contribute to real-world software projects, collaborate with experienced professionals, and build reliable, scalable, and user-friendly applications that solve meaningful problems.

I believe in learning by doing, improving every day, and turning ideas into working solutions.
